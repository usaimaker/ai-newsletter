Issue 2026-10-23 · Practical AI tools that save money & time

Five fresh, practical moves this week that cut cost or effort in your AI workflows — all based on real releases reported in the last few weeks.

1. Claude Haiku 5.5: the new floor for high-volume agents

Cost

Anthropic Haiku 5.5 lands at $0.10 in / $0.50 out per million tokens — about 90% below Haiku 4.5 and tied with GPT-6 Luna as the cheapest production-grade small model. For agents that make thousands of tiny LLM calls (classification, extraction, routing), this is the biggest cost lever you control. Watch-out: prompts over 100K tokens are billed at 5x, so keep routine calls short.

2. Free ChatGPT tier now runs GPT-6 Luna; mid-tier price parity

Free tier

GPT-6 Luna is available at $0 inside the ChatGPT free tier, while the paid mid-tier converged: GPT-6.1 Sol and Gemini 4 Argon both list at $2 / $10 per million with cached input at $0.10. For builders, the cache price matters more than the headline — agents that resend context every step now save roughly 20% on most workloads.

3. DeepSeek Search + Fetch APIs are free until Oct 31

Free this month

DeepSeek opened its in-house Search and Fetch APIs to all developers at $0.75 per 1,000 calls — and they are free until 2026-10-31. Wire them into your research agents this month to get web search and page extraction without paying a separate search provider, then decide before the cutoff.

4. AutoTask AI: autonomous tasks with an error-recovery layer

Time-saver

The October launch of AutoTask AI runs natural-language tasks across 47 native integrations (calendar, email, CRM, PM) and an open API. Its differentiator is the error-recovery layer: when a subtask fails it tries a resolution path and only escalates when truly stuck. The free tier covers 10 tasks/month — enough to automate one real daily routine at no cost.

5. LayerFS 1.0: a filesystem built for parallel agents

Open source

LayerFS 1.0 (MIT, 1,650+ stars) calls itself "the substrate under your agents": each agent mounts a complete writable workspace over shared committed history, commits its changes, and exits — so work can branch, rewind, or merge. Self-host it to coordinate a multi-agent fleet without writing your own git-and-locks glue code.

Automation tip of the day
Stack cheap small models with prompt caching for high-volume agent loops. Keep each Haiku 5.5 prompt under 100K tokens (avoid the 5x surcharge) and cache the repeated context your agent resends every step — that combination typically cuts an agent token bill by ~20% versus re-sending fresh each turn.

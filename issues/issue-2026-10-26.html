Five Practical AI Automation Wins for October 26, 2026

The cost of producing AI work keeps falling while new tools hand routine jobs to software you barely configure. Each item saves money or time, often both.

IrisGo for Solopreneurs

Backed by Andrew Ng AI Fund, IrisGo hit Product of the Day on Product Hunt on October 7, 2026. Its Watch and Learn feature records a multi-step admin routine you demonstrate once, then repeats it on new inputs, free during public beta on Windows 11 and Apple Silicon with data kept on device. Automate expense filing, weekly reports, or inbox triage without building an integration.

OpenAI Decisions API

In public beta since October 6, 2026, it returns typed verdicts from text and images instead of prose, about ten times faster than the Responses API. Billed at roughly 0.10 per million input tokens with no output or cache charges in beta, ideal for routing, moderation, and branching logic.

Inception Mercury 3

Launched October 19, 2026, this diffusion LLM generates tokens in parallel and claims 1,068 tokens per second on NVIDIA GPUs, with a one-million-token context and OpenAI-API compatibility. Launch pricing half off at 0.10 per million input and 0.35 per million output, new keys get 100 million free tokens.

Claude Haiku 5.5

Released October 7, 2026 at 0.10 per million input and 0.50 per million output with a one-million-token context, the cheapest frontier small model. Route your highest-frequency calls here and reserve flagships for the few tasks that need them.

Notion sixth-generation agents

Rolled out October 20, 2026, Notion can now run a single agent task for over 24 hours and added an Auto model router that picks the best-cost model per step. Long-running automations no longer need oversized token windows, and admins get spending controls.

Automation tip of the day

Route the boring 90 percent to cheap models and reserve the frontier for the hard 10 percent. Build a thin routing layer: classification, extraction, and formatting to Haiku 5.5 or Mercury 3, only genuine reasoning to a flagship, and the Decisions API for any yes or no branch. Multi-vendor setups typically cut a single-vendor bill by 40 to 60 percent with no quality loss.
